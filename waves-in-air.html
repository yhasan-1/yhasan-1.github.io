Waves in Air

Solo project | Godot and GDScript | Three months

Godot GDScript Event-Driven Programming Game Design Pathfinding

A narrative puzzle game about communication, conflict, and community. Players deliver letters between people and solve environmental puzzles along the way.

Made for my UMD Honors Humanities Keystone project. I handled the research, writing, concept art, puzzle design, and development.

Gameplay

The Radio Station

A radio operator refuses a letter from the player. Outside the station, dried flowers and overgrown vines hint at how the place has been neglected. Watering the flowers and clearing the vines makes the operator receptive to the delivery. The puzzle explores communication through actions rather than dialogue alone.

Development

Event System

Built custom Godot signals to connect interactions, puzzle completion, and story progression.

Click-to-Move Navigation

Prototyped pathfinding in a test scene, then adjusted navigation regions and spacing to fix movement issues.

Scope and Story

Cut an ambitious fictional-language system to focus on environmental puzzles. Reworked the narrative after mentor feedback, refining the theme alongside the mechanics.

Research and Influences

Research by Raymond Cohen and Boaz Keysar informed the communication themes. Chants of Sennaar and 7 Days to End with You inspired the early language-puzzle concept; VA-11 HALL-A and Dredge influenced the writing and pacing.

Result

Completed a playable game and a narrative write-up. Communication remained the central theme, while the story and puzzles brought empathy and understanding others' circumstances into sharper focus.
